Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Your privacy and data security are important to us.

1. Information We Collect

When you use UCC Hostel Finder, unlock hostel manager details, or submit inquiries through our forms, we collect the following essential information:

  • Personal Contact Information: Full name, email address, and phone number provided during payment checkout or inquiry submissions.
  • Transaction Details: Payment transaction reference IDs, date/time of unlock, and the selected hostel details.
  • Usage Data: Basic site navigation data, search inputs, and location filter interactions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to facilitate hostel searching and management services, including:

  • Processing your GH₵ 35.00 contact unlock transaction via our payment gateway (Paystack).
  • Unlocking and displaying verified hostel manager details for your requested accommodation.
  • Providing customer care and WhatsApp support assistance.
  • Improving platform search accuracy, hostel listings, and overall user experience.

3. Payment Security & Financial Data

We prioritize your financial safety and security:

  • No Financial Storage: UCC Hostel Finder does NOT collect, process, or store sensitive Mobile Money PINs, credit/debit card numbers, or bank login details on our servers.
  • Third-Party Payment Gateway: All financial payments are securely handled by Paystack, a licensed payment provider using industry-standard PCI-DSS encryption.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. We only share information under strict necessity:

  • With payment processors (Paystack) to complete transaction verification.
  • With hostel managers when necessary to facilitate direct student booking follow-ups.
  • If legally required by law enforcement or regulatory authorities.

5. Cookies and Web Analytics

Our website uses basic browser storage and cookies to save your user preferences, support dynamic location search filters, and enhance site responsiveness on mobile devices.

6. Your Data Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate information, or ask for your records to be deleted from our inquiry databases.
