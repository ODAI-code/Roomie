3. Contact Unlock Fee (GH₵ 35.00)

  • The GH₵ 35.00 fee paid on this website is strictly a Service & Contact Unlock Fee.
  • This payment unlocks direct contact details (phone number, WhatsApp, email) and direct booking/payment channels for the respective hostel manager.
  • IMPORTANT: The GH₵ 50.00 payment does NOT constitute hostel rent, a security deposit, or a booking deposit for the hostel room. Hostel rent must be settled directly with the hostel manager.

4. Payments & Refunds

All online transactions are processed securely via Paystack. Once manager contact information is successfully unlocked and displayed on your screen, the unlock fee is generally non-refundable.

If a listed hostel is verified to be completely unavailable at the exact time of unlock, users may contact support to transfer their unlock fee credit to another listed hostel.

5. User Responsibilities & Due Diligence

While UCC Hostel Finder takes steps to curate and maintain accurate listing information, students and users are strongly advised to:

  • Inspect the physical room and facilities in person (or through a trusted representative) before transferring full hostel rent.
  • Verify the identity of the hostel manager and confirm room availability directly.
  • Ensure official receipts are issued by the hostel manager upon paying room rent.
